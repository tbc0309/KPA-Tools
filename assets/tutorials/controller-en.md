### Step 0 — Configure the controller (important)

1. Open Device Settings (AYASetting), select Controller, change the controller style to Xbox, and calibrate the controller.

   With the original AYANEO controller style, ABXY may stop working in RetroArch after the device wakes from standby.

2. Press the KONKR button at the lower right to open the quick panel. Open Controller and set ABXY Mode to Japanese.

3. Open Android Settings → System → Gestures → System navigation → Gesture navigation. Select the gear beside Gesture navigation and turn off “Swipe to invoke the assistant.”
